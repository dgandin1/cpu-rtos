## Register Layout

x0  : zero  the constant value 0
x1  : ra    return address
x2  : sp    stack pointer
x3  : gp    global pointers
x4  : tp    thread pointer
x5-x7       temporaries (caller saved)
x8  :       saved register 
x9  :       callee saved register
x10-x11     function arguments/return values
x12-x17     function arguments
x18-27      saved registers (callee)
x28-31      temporary registers (caller)

## Supported RISC-V Instruction Details

The following RISC-V instuctions are supported:

 - ADD, ADDI, SUB
 - AND, OR, XOR
 - BEQ, BNE, BLT, BGE
 - JAL, JALR
 - LW, SW
 - SRA, SRL

## Instruction Formats

#### ADD

 31      25 24    20 19    15 14  12 11    7 6      0
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+
 |        
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+

 - rd <= rs1 + rs2

 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+

 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+

#### SUB

 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+

 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+

 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+

#### XOR

 - rd <= rs1 ^ rs2

 31      25 24    20 19    15 14  12 11    7 6      0
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+
 |0000000  | rs2    | rs1    | 100  | rd    | 0110011|

#### ADDI

 - rd <= rs1 + imm

 31               20 19    15 14  12 11    7 6      0
 +------------------+--------+------+-------+--------+
 |imm               | rs1    | 000  | rd    | 0010011|
 +------------------+--------+------+-------+--------+

#### BEQ

 - PC = (rs1 == rs2) ? PC + imm : PC + 4

 31      25 24    20 19    15 14  12 11    7 6      0
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+
 |imm      | rs2    | rs1    | 000  | imm   | 1100011|        
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+

#### BNE

 - PC = (rs1 != rs2) ? PC + imm : PC + 4

 31      25 24    20 19    15 14  12 11    7 6      0
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+
 |imm      | rs2    | rs1    | 001  | imm   | 1100011|         
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+

#### BLT

 - PC = (rs1 < rs2) ? PC + imm : PC + 4

 31      25 24    20 19    15 14  12 11    7 6      0
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+
 |imm      | rs2    | rs1    | 100  | imm   | 1100011|         
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+

#### BGE

 - PC = (rs1 > rs2) ? PC + imm : PC + 4

 31      25 24    20 19    15 14  12 11    7 6      0
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+
 |imm      | rs2    | rs1    | 101  | imm   | 1100011|         
 +---------+--------+--------+------+-------+--------+


#### JAL

 - rd = PC + 4; PC = PC + imm

 31                               12 11    7 6      0
 +----------------------------------+-------+--------+
 |imm                               | rd    | 1101111|         
 +----------------------------------+-------+--------+
